# DBM-AlertCustomizer
Tweak with the announcements, and special announcements in (Color, Msg, Sounds).

This mod was created for myself because I wanted to tweak with the announcement colors as I wish, with that in mind this plugin-addon was born.

The way it works is very simple, write /dbmcolor or /dbmalert or /dbmalerts --- All does the same thing, a GUI will open up.

Make sure to load the boss mod from DBM, otherwise the list will be empty. To do this you either go into the dungeon/instance or near a world boss, OR for ease just open up /dbm and go to Raids/Dungeon tabs (whichever you want to modify) then click the raid-dungeon area you wish you tweak.

Once boss mod is loaded and DBM-AlertCustomizer GUI is opened, just minimize any raid that you want to modify its alerts, go to the boss. Check for which ability you want, you'd see there a list of abilities either for everyone, or near you, or abilities ON YOU.

Next is kind of self-explanatory. You will see next to each ability a box to change its COLOR, a box to change its MSG, another box to change its sound. Please note that if you wish you keep the default DBM sound + add your sound, both can work together. Otherwise you can in the sound box to DISABLE DBM Soundfile and just keep whatever you choose, you can also choose to play anything as a Text-to-speak.
