Disclaimer: This addon does nothing without DBM, you need to have DBM on and installed for it to work.

Command /dbmalert

Features

Change the type any ability announcement to special announcement, or special announcement to announcement. Can also choose if you want it to be Special1, 2, 3, 4 (based off DBM options settings).
Change the color of any announcenemtn or special announcement
Change the MSG itself to say whatever you want for the ability.
Change the sound for any ability, you can add from sharedmedia or write your own. (could keep DBM sound + add your own sound, or disable DBM and keep your own only.)

In detail explanation if you like to read:

Tweak with the announcements, and special announcements in (Color, Msg, Sounds).

This mod was created for myself because I wanted to tweak with the announcement colors as I wish, with that in mind this plugin-addon was born.

Disclaimer: THIS MOD/plugin DOES NOTHING WITHOUT DBM addon.

The way it works is very simple, write /dbmcolor or /dbmalert or /dbmalerts --- All does the same thing, a GUI will open up.

Make sure to load the boss mod from DBM, otherwise the list will be empty. To do this you either go into the dungeon/instance or near a world boss, OR for ease just open up /dbm and go to Raids/Dungeon tabs (whichever you want to modify) then click the raid-dungeon area you wish you tweak.

Once boss mod is loaded and DBM-AlertCustomizer GUI is opened, just expand any raid that you want to modify its alerts, go to the boss. Check for which ability you want, you'd see there a list of abilities either for everyone, or near you, or abilities ON YOU.

Next is kind of self-explanatory. You will see next to each ability a box to change its COLOR, a box to change its MSG, another box to change its sound. Please note that if you wish you keep the default DBM sound + add your sound, both can work together. Otherwise you can DISABLE DBM Soundfile and just keep whatever you choose, you can also choose to play anything as a Text-to-speak, all of this is done inside of the Sound box menu.
